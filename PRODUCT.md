# Price X-Ray (@PriceXRay) — Product Context

## Product Summary
**Price X-Ray** (`@PriceXRay`) is a forensic consumer-advocacy media brand and interactive OEM Factory Twin bio-storefront. It exposes the exact Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and Original Design Manufacturers (ODMs) behind high-markup retail brands, pairing each 45-second video teardown with verified, unblurred links to the identical-spec Factory Twin for 60%–90% less.

## Core Tagline & Brand Voice
- **Tagline:** *"Same Factory. Same Specs. 80% Less. We X-Ray the Markup."*
- **Secondary Hooks:**
  - *"Don't Pay the Logo Tax. Buy the Factory Twin."*
  - *"Exposing Brand Markups — One Factory Code at a Time."*
- **Voice:** Forensic, authoritative, transparent, specification-driven, consumer-first. Uses real regulatory filings, patent numbers, metallurgy grades (e.g., SUS304, VG10, 6061-T6), and component model numbers (e.g., Qualcomm QCC3091, 110,000 RPM BLDC, WPC Qi2 MPP).

## Target Audience & Usage Scene
- **Primary Audience:** High-intent mobile viewers arriving directly from YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels via the `@PriceXRay` bio link, plus desktop researchers comparing product specifications.
- **Usage Scene:** Rapid episode lookup (`#1`–`#100` or brand/category search) under variable ambient lighting where dark-mode contrast, instant scannability, tabular price math, and one-tap multi-network checkout (Amazon Prime, AliExpress Factory Direct, Walmart) are critical.
